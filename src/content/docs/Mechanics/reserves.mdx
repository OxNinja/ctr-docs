---
title: Reserves
---

A hidden mechanics in the sense that players are not meant to see the reserves' value itself.

Reserves are the amount of "fuel" you have left to use to boost or maintain a great speed. Your reserves amount decreases each frame. The only ways to gain some is by [MT](/mechanics/mt), drifting on a boost pad or use a turbo item.

Some says that there are 2 states for a character, regarding reserves:

- Spending

    When you do "nothing", reserves are just lost, unused. Or hit a special turbo pad, you will "burn" them in exchange of great speed.

- Earning

    As said above, with [MT](/mechanics/mt), drifting on turbo pads or using a turbo item one can stack reserves, in order to spend them.
