---
title: Mini Turbo
---

Initiate a drift by jumping and holding a direction when landing, hold the jump button untill you want to cancel the drift.

Within a second or so, your exhaust smoke turns black, and the bar at the bottom-right of the screen fills. Press the oposite jump button when the bar indicator is red to activate a mini turbo (MT). You can chain up to 3 MT in the same drift.

An MT give a little speed boost and fills a little bit your reserves. Ensure to fill the drift bar indicator as much as possible (also called full MT, or FMT) to get better speed.
