---
title: Utlimate Sacred Fire
---

There are multiple speed levels in this game, no fire being the slowest and ultimate sacred fire (USF) being the fastest.

USF can be achieved by driving on a special turbo pad, which can be found on every track of the 4th and last hub of the adventure mode (N. Gin Labs, Cortex Castle, Hot Air Skyway and Oxide Station).

Those pads forces you to empty your reserves, which is great. The more reserves you have, the longer you will keep the USF.

The following actions will cancel your USF:

- [MT](/mechanics/mt)
- Hitting a wall
- Driving on a turbo pad
- Using a turbo item
- Braking
- Getting a hangtime turbo
- Getting hit by and item
