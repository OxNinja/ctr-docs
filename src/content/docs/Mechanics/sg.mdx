---
title: Speed Ghost
---

CTR's physics gives a speed boost when driving on downhills, this is what we call speed ghost (SG). Technically, you could even get SG from any unflat surface.

We try to minimize air time on downhills to get as much speed as possible. Usually, we also combine an SG with [froggy](/mechanics/froggy) if possible, to carry the speed for as long as possible.
